## 🎨 Design Highlights

### Colors Used
- **Green**: #84c225 (primary actions)
- **Red**: #cc0000 (checkout, remove)
- **Gray**: #f8f8f8 (backgrounds)
- **Dark**: #333333 (text)

### Shadow System
- **Subtle**: `0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`
- **Normal**: `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`
- **Deep**: `0 8px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`

### Spacing Grid
- Base: 8px
- Common: 16px, 24px, 32px
